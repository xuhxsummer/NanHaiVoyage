# Part B — Visible-horizon sea traffic (locked with Summer 2026-09-09)

## Intent
On the **main voyage sea**, pirates and other ships (shop hull types) are **visible**. Traffic is occasional. Pirates are dangerous but avoidable. Merchants live their own loop; player may plunder. Only ships **the player sinks** yield loot (auto-pickup).

## Visible sea ≠ camera pan
- **Horizon visibility** = distance from **player ship** (sea-plane), NOT whether the 3D camera currently looks at them.
- Dragging/looking away and back must **not** despawn pirates.
- Pirates despawn only when player **sails** beyond horizon distance.
- Merchant NPCs **persist** when out of horizon and keep simulating; player can re-encounter them later.

## Caps / frequency
- Typical on-horizon presence: about **0–1 pirate** + **0–1 merchant** (low frequency; merchants must not snowball wealth).

## Pirates
- **Visible** 3D (same ship rendering family as player/pirate art).
- **Stationary** (no chase by default). Heading may face player when in fire range.
- Spawn **outside** fire range but **inside** horizon so player can see and divert.
- **Aggro B**: if player enters fire range, pirate **opens fire** without waiting for lock. Lock still used for player return fire / focus.
- **Per-ship cannon damage**: on spawn roll **integer 1–10 once**; that pirate keeps that damage for its life (not re-roll every shot). Replace fixed `PIRATE_SHOT = 1`.
- NPC merchants in range: pirates may attack them; merchants **fight back**; outcome by stats.
- Despawn when `dist(player, pirate) > horizon` (sail away).

### Locked range numbers (world units)
| Constant | Old | New |
|---|---|---|
| Spawn distance from player | ~300 | **800–1000** (random in band) |
| `PIRATE_RANGE` (fire) | 430 | **650–700** (pick one, e.g. 680) |
| Horizon despawn (was `PIRATE_FLEE_RANGE` 540) | 540 | **1100–1200** (e.g. 1150); rename/document as horizon clear for pirates |

Auto-sail may still graze fire range under B (player accepted). Keep 0.28.14 behavior: auto-sail **does not cancel** on pirate spawn; combat can still damage if course enters range.

## Merchant / other ships
- Hull from **shop ship roster** (`Catalog` ships); **HP / cannon damage scale with ship type** (`SHIP_FIRE` etc. + sensible hull).
- Low-frequency spawn in horizon band (similar spawn distances), then **AI loop**: sail between ports, light trade (small silver/cargo gains, capped), explore islands rarely, engage pirates if threatened.
- **Do not attack player** unless player **locks** them (plunder = same lock UX as pirates). Once locked/in combat with player, they **return fire**.
- Player may also lock/fight pirates while merchants fight pirates — free-for-all OK; or sit out and watch.
- **Loot**: auto-grant cargo/silver/etc. **only if the player delivers the killing blow** (player’s shot sinks them). If pirate sinks merchant (or merchant sinks pirate), **no player loot**.
- On sink: entity removed (no wreck pickup).
- Out of horizon: **keep simulating** off-screen (throttle OK); can reappear when player approaches again.
