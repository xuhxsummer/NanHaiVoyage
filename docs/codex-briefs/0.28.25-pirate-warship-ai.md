## Plain goal (user)
Make combat feel less “ram straight into my bow and glue”:
1. Locked pirate/warship **circle to the side** and fight at gun range — not face-plant into the player.
2. At **≤50% HP**, **turn away and withdraw** while still returning fire (not random mirror flee).
3. **Pirate fiercer / closer**; **warship steadier / more standoff** (keep existing probabilistic ram for warships).

Keep merchant behavior: flee + return fire when hostile/hit (do not give merchants chase).

---

## 1) Flank chase + hold fire band (locked)
When pirate is `combatLock` (and warship when hostile/locked equivalent from 0.28.24):

- Do **not** steer straight at player center as the only goal.
- Steer toward a **flank point**: offset beside the player along a perpendicular to the player→enemy vector (alternate port/starboard every few seconds or when blocked), at a preferred distance inside cannon range.
- Preferred distance band: about **60%–90% of `Catalog.PIRATE_RANGE`** (tune). Outside band → close in; inside too-close (near hull radii) → back off / slide sideways.
- While chasing: move + fire when in range and roughly aimed; avoid “ram while shooting” as the only tactic.
- Unlock: optional short continue-chase / last volley for ~2–3s, then resume idle cruise (do not instantly freeze).

Files: `GameState.updateCombat`, pirate move helpers, warship update path (`WarshipData` / `updateWarship`).

## 2) ≤50% HP: withdraw toward open water
When pirate or warship HP ≤ 50% max:

- Switch from flank-chase to **withdraw**: heading roughly **away from the player**, prefer open water (avoid driving into port/island hitboxes if cheap to check).
- Keep **return fire** while fleeing (same as merchant flee+fire spirit).
- Do not only use a naïve mirror-point jitter that looks like stuck sliding.

Above 50% again (if healed — usually N/A) or if not low: locked → flank chase as §1.

## 3) Role differentiation
- **Pirate**: higher chase aggression, preferred band closer to player (lower end of 60–90% range), more willing to close.
- **Warship**: preferred band farther (upper end), slightly slower close-in; keep **probabilistic ram** from 0.28.22 when in opportunity range.
- Merchants/traders: unchanged flee+fire; never chase.

## 4) Collision synergy
Chase goal points stay on the **flank**, so mutual push from 0.28.24 is less constantly fighting a head-on seek. Do not remove mutual separation.
